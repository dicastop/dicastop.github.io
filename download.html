How to Download Free Craft Patterns from Ver e Fazer and Save Them on Pinterest or Google Drive

If you enjoy crafts, sewing, felt projects, EVA crafts, crochet, embroidery, or printable templates, Ver e Fazer offers a collection of free ideas, tutorials, and craft patterns. In this guide, you will learn how to find a pattern, save it to your device, organize your favorite ideas on Pinterest, and store permitted files in Google Drive.

How to Find a Pattern on Ver e Fazer

Start by visiting Ver e Fazer and browse through the available categories or use the website's search function to find a specific project.

You can find different types of craft content, including:

  • Felt patterns
  • EVA craft patterns
  • Crochet projects
  • Sewing patterns
  • Printable templates
  • PDF patterns
  • Children's activities
  • Decorations and creative crafts

How to Download a Pattern

Step 1: Open the Pattern

Open the Ver e Fazer article containing the craft project you want to use. Read the instructions and look for the pattern image or an available download option.

Step 2: Save an Image Pattern

If the pattern is displayed as an image and downloading it is permitted, move your mouse over the image and use the available options to save it.

On a computer, you can usually right-click the image and select Save image as.... Choose a folder and give the file a descriptive name, such as:

  • felt-flower-pattern.jpg
  • eva-butterfly-pattern.png
  • christmas-craft-pattern.jpg

Step 3: Download a PDF

If the article provides a PDF or another downloadable file, use the corresponding download link and save the file to your computer or mobile device.

Some Ver e Fazer projects may provide downloadable materials through external storage services such as Google Drive.

How to Save Ver e Fazer Patterns on Pinterest

Pinterest is useful for organizing craft inspiration into themed boards. Instead of keeping dozens of links scattered across your browser, you can create boards based on the type of project.

For example, you could create boards such as:

  • Felt Crafts
  • Sewing Patterns
  • EVA Crafts
  • Crochet Ideas
  • Christmas Crafts
  • Children's Crafts
  • Printable Patterns

Save the Original Ver e Fazer Page

Whenever possible, use Pinterest to save the original Ver e Fazer article rather than downloading and re-uploading someone else's images.

Open the article, use Pinterest's save option if available, and select the board where you want to keep the project.

This approach makes it easier to return to the original tutorial whenever you want to make the project.

How to Save Patterns in Google Drive

Google Drive is especially useful for organizing permitted personal copies of PDF files and other craft materials.

Step 1: Open Google Drive

Visit Google Drive and sign in to your Google account.

Step 2: Create a Folder

Click New and create a folder called Ver e Fazer Patterns, or choose another name that makes sense for your collection.

You can create additional folders inside it, for example:

  • Felt
  • EVA
  • Sewing
  • Crochet
  • Christmas
  • Children's Crafts
  • PDF Patterns

Step 3: Upload Your Files

Open the appropriate folder, click New → File upload, and select the permitted pattern files from your computer.

You can also drag files directly into the Google Drive window.

Step 4: Rename Your Files

Descriptive filenames make your collection much easier to search. For example:

  • flower-felt-pattern.pdf
  • christmas-eva-pattern.png
  • baby-sewing-pattern.jpg

Respect Copyright and Usage Restrictions

Before downloading, sharing, or uploading a pattern to another platform, check the usage conditions associated with the material.

Saving a permitted file for your own personal use is different from copying and redistributing the same material on another website, social network, public folder, or file-sharing service.

When possible, save the original Ver e Fazer article to Pinterest and keep the source information associated with your personal files. This helps preserve attribution and makes it easier to return to the original tutorial.

A Simple Way to Organize Your Craft Collection

  1. Find the project: Search Ver e Fazer for the pattern or tutorial you want.
  2. Save the original page: Add it to an appropriate Pinterest board.
  3. Download permitted files: Save an image or PDF when the usage terms allow it.
  4. Upload your personal copy: Store it in the appropriate Google Drive folder.
  5. Rename the file: Use a descriptive filename.
  6. Keep the source: Save the original article address with your notes or file information.

Final Thoughts

Saving and organizing craft patterns can be simple when you use the right tools. Ver e Fazer can be used to discover craft ideas and tutorials, Pinterest can help you organize inspiration, and Google Drive can keep permitted personal files organized and accessible from different devices.

Always respect the copyright and usage conditions associated with each pattern. When in doubt, save the original article as a bookmark or Pinterest Pin instead of re-uploading the material elsewhere.
